The agent studies your demo trades only. It looks for patterns that lost money and suggests rules to avoid them. Past results do not guarantee future results: a rule that fits the last 30 trades can fail on the next 30, so treat low-confidence findings as hints and keep collecting trades. It never places real orders. Apply works when this page and the scanner open from the same place (the same folder on your computer, or the same GitHub Pages site).
