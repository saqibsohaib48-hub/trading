How the spot demo works: spot cannot short, so it only takes READY long signals (shorts go to the futures demo). Every trade first passes the 9-check pre-trade gate; any FAIL skips it and two or more warnings halve the size. It buys at the live ask plus slippage, never the old scan price, sized so the stop costs your risk % including fees, but never more than 25% of equity or more than your free cash (spot has no leverage). At TP1 (1.5R) it sells 40% and moves the stop to breakeven after fees; at TP2 (2.5R) another 30% and the stop moves to TP1; at TP3 (4R) the rest. Take-profits fill only when price trades through them; a stop that price gaps past fills at the worse live price. Trades close only at a take-profit or the stop (or your Close). Binance spot fee is 0.1% per side. The demo runs only while this page is open and is saved in this browser.

How the futures demo works, like a Binance USDⓈ-M isolated-margin position: entries are market orders filled at the live futures ask (long) or bid (short) plus your extra slippage, and pay the taker fee. The stop keeps the signal's distance in %, and size is set so that hitting the stop, including fees and slippage, costs your risk % of the balance. Every trade first passes the 9-check pre-trade gate (portfolio heat, position size, correlated exposure, BTC regime, volume, stop-loss size, risk:reward after fees, revenge trading, daily loss limit): any FAIL skips the trade and two or more warnings halve it. Size is also capped by max total exposure (3× equity by default) and to 25% of the visible order book. Targets are 1.5R/2.5R/4R closing 40/30/30%, and the stop moves to breakeven after fees at TP1. Take-profits are limit orders that fill only when price trades through the target, with the maker fee; stops are stop-market orders: they fill at the stop price, or at the worse live price if price gaps past it, with double slippage and the taker fee; a manual Close is a market order at the live bid/ask with slippage and the taker fee. Trades only close at a take-profit or the stop-loss, never on a timer or a signal change. Funding is charged or paid at each real funding time using the live rate and mark price (longs pay when the rate is positive). Liquidation uses the mark price and an estimated maintenance margin; if it is hit, the position's whole margin is lost. A signal is skipped if its stop sits beyond the liquidation price or there isn't enough free margin. Maintenance margin rates and maximum leverage vary by coin and position size on Binance, so check your own limits before trading live.

How the scanner decides

Every coin goes through up to 29 scoring checks across four timeframes (5m, 15m, 1h, 4h). Each check votes long or short with a weight, and the votes become a 0–100 score for each side. Coins without futures skip the 6 futures-based checks.

  • Trend (9): 4h trend, 1h trend, 1h EMA200, 15m EMA 9/21, 15m market structure (higher highs/lows), 15m Supertrend, 15m ADX trend strength, BTC direction, strength vs BTC over 4h.
  • Momentum (9): 5m EMA cross, 5m MACD, 5m RSI, 1h RSI, 5m Stoch RSI, price vs VWAP, 5m Bollinger breakout/squeeze, 15m RSI divergence, 5m candle pattern (engulfing, hammer, shooting star).
  • Volume & order flow (4): 5m volume spike, taker buy/sell ratio, 15m OBV trend, order book depth within 1%.
  • Whales (2): large trades from the latest 1,000 (0.01% of 24h volume, minimum $20k), and positions of the biggest Binance futures accounts.
  • Futures (4): funding rate, open interest change, futures taker buy/sell, retail long/short ratio (read as a crowding signal).
  • Context (1): Fear & Greed. Market-cap rank and CoinGecko trending appear as tags.

29-indicator confirmation (your list): Supertrend, Ichimoku Cloud, RSI, MACD, VWAP, ADX/DMI, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic RSI, EMA 9/21/50/200, Volume Profile, ATR, Parabolic SAR, CCI, Williams %R, Keltner Channels, Donchian Channels, Hull MA, Average Volume, Pivot Points Standard, OBV, plus the direction indicators UT Bot Alerts, AlphaTrend, HalfTrend, QQE MOD, WaveTrend, SSL Hybrid, Buy/Sell Volume Pressure, TradingView Technical Ratings and Lorentzian Classification (Supertrend is in both lists and counts once). Each votes BUY, SELL or NEUTRAL, mostly on the 15m chart (Ichimoku on 1h, Pivots on the daily, VWAP on 5m). Every trade records every vote, so each indicator builds its own win %; higher win % counts more. They confirm the score rather than adding to it.

READY needs a score at or above your threshold (default 70) and must pass all 10 safety gates, starting with the S1+S2 main confirmation: volatility in a tradable range, RSI not stretched, tight spread, volume or whales confirming, room to reach TP1 (1.5R) before the 24h high/low, not overextended on the day (±20%), enough order book liquidity, enough of the 20 confirmation indicators agreeing, and for shorts, the coin must have Binance futures (spot-only coins can't be shorted). A high score that fails a gate shows as WATCH.

Main confirmation: S1 + S2 (your TradingView script). S1 compares the live price with yesterday’s daily close: green allows longs only, red allows shorts only. S2 needs a significant move (more than 0.4% from yesterday’s close) and price on the right side of the weighted line’s entry zone (SMA 10 of close × (1 − difference) on 15m bars, ±0.15%). No trade is allowed without all three. Two fixes to the original script: it used '1430', which is a 1430-minute bar, not a daily one, so this uses the real daily close; and its dynamic threshold compared a percent to a price, which made it always true on cheap coins and never true on BTC, so the 0.4% threshold now applies to every coin.

Levels: stop loss sits beyond the recent 1-hour swing on the 5m chart, limited to 0.5–2.5× ATR. Targets are 1.5R, 2.5R and 4R, closing 40/30/30%: that keeps risk:reward above 1.5 after fees, which the old 1R/2R/3R targets did not. Signals use closed candles only, so they don’t flip mid-candle.

Position size uses your account size and risk %, so hitting the stop loses only that percentage.

Signals are rule-based readings of public market data, not financial advice and not a guarantee of profit. Scalping is high risk, fees and slippage add up, and data can lag. Check the chart yourself before every trade and never risk money you can’t afford to lose.
