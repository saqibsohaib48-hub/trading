How the demo works: when a scan marks a coin READY, the demo opens a trade at the scan price, sized so the stop loss costs your risk % (set in Settings) of the demo balance. Prices are checked every 5 seconds. At TP1 it closes 50% and moves the stop to entry. At TP2 it closes another 30% and moves the stop to TP1. At TP3 it closes the rest. A trade only ever closes at a take-profit or the stop-loss (or when you press Close); there is no time limit and no close on signal changes. Fees are charged on every fill. Max 10× of balance per trade, one trade per coin, and a 15-minute pause on a coin after it closes. The demo only runs while this page is open, and it is saved in this browser. Real fills can be worse than these results because of slippage and very fast moves.

How the futures demo works, like a Binance USDⓈ-M isolated-margin position: entries are market orders filled at the live futures ask (long) or bid (short) plus your extra slippage, and pay the taker fee. The stop keeps the signal's distance in %, and size is set so that hitting the stop, including fees and slippage, costs your risk % of the balance. Take-profits are limit orders filled at the target price with the maker fee; stops are stop-market orders resting on the exchange, so they fill at the stop price with double slippage and the taker fee; a manual Close is a market order at the live bid/ask with slippage and the taker fee. Trades only close at a take-profit or the stop-loss, never on a timer or a signal change. Funding is charged or paid at each real funding time using the live rate and mark price (longs pay when the rate is positive). Liquidation uses the mark price and an estimated maintenance margin; if it is hit, the position's whole margin is lost. A signal is skipped if its stop sits beyond the liquidation price or there isn't enough free margin. Maintenance margin rates and maximum leverage vary by coin and position size on Binance, so check your own limits before trading live.

How the scanner decides

Every coin goes through up to 29 scoring checks across four timeframes (5m, 15m, 1h, 4h). Each check votes long or short with a weight, and the votes become a 0–100 score for each side. Coins without futures skip the 6 futures-based checks.

  • Trend (9): 4h trend, 1h trend, 1h EMA200, 15m EMA 9/21, 15m market structure (higher highs/lows), 15m Supertrend, 15m ADX trend strength, BTC direction, strength vs BTC over 4h.
  • Momentum (9): 5m EMA cross, 5m MACD, 5m RSI, 1h RSI, 5m Stoch RSI, price vs VWAP, 5m Bollinger breakout/squeeze, 15m RSI divergence, 5m candle pattern (engulfing, hammer, shooting star).
  • Volume & order flow (4): 5m volume spike, taker buy/sell ratio, 15m OBV trend, order book depth within 1%.
  • Whales (2): large trades from the latest 1,000 (0.01% of 24h volume, minimum $20k), and positions of the biggest Binance futures accounts.
  • Futures (4): funding rate, open interest change, futures taker buy/sell, retail long/short ratio (read as a crowding signal).
  • Context (1): Fear & Greed. Market-cap rank and CoinGecko trending appear as tags.

20-indicator confirmation: EMA 9/21/50, RSI, MACD, Supertrend, VWAP, Bollinger Bands, ATR, ADX, Stochastic, Ichimoku Cloud, Fibonacci retracement, Parabolic SAR, SMA 50/200, Stochastic RSI, CCI, OBV, Donchian Channels, Keltner Channels, daily Pivot Points and Williams %R. Each votes BUY, SELL or NEUTRAL, mostly on the 15m chart (Ichimoku, Fibonacci and SMA on 1h, Pivots on the daily). They confirm the score rather than adding to it, so the same signal isn't counted twice.

READY needs a score at or above your threshold (default 70) and must pass all 9 safety gates: volatility in a tradable range, RSI not stretched, tight spread, volume or whales confirming, room to reach TP1 before the 24h high/low, not overextended on the day (±30%), enough order book liquidity, enough of the 20 confirmation indicators agreeing, and for shorts, the coin must have Binance futures (spot-only coins can't be shorted). A high score that fails a gate shows as WATCH.

Levels: stop loss sits beyond the recent 1-hour swing on the 5m chart, limited to 0.5–2.5× ATR. Targets are 1R, 2R and 3R. A common way to manage it: take part profit at TP1 and move the stop to entry.

Position size uses your account size and risk %, so hitting the stop loses only that percentage.

Signals are rule-based readings of public market data, not financial advice and not a guarantee of profit. Scalping is high risk, fees and slippage add up, and data can lag. Check the chart yourself before every trade and never risk money you can’t afford to lose.
